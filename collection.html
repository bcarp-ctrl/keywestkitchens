The line

Every cabinet a kitchen needs, sized to your drawing.

Key West Kitchens is organized the way a cabinet spec guide is organized. Every family below is built to the same ¾″ maple-plywood, full-access construction and sized in 1/16″ increments. Nominal codes are for reference; the width, height and depth on your approved drawings are what we build.

1 · WALL

Wall cabinets

Single and double door, glass-ready, open, corner (blind and diagonal), microwave, over-fridge, two-section uppers. Tall uppers are built as one cabinet with two door sections, never stacked boxes, in custom layouts to your drawing. 12″ deep standard; 13″–24″ on request.

2 · BASE

Base cabinets

Door, door + drawer, 2-, 3- and 4-drawer stacks, sink bases, blind corners, lazy-susan corners, trash pull-outs, appliance bases. 24″ deep standard with full tops; 30½″ box + 4″ toe = 34½″.

3 · TALL

Tall cabinets

Pantries, oven and microwave towers, broom closets, refrigerator surrounds. 84″, 90″ and 96″ heights or custom; 12″–27″ deep.

4 · VANITY

Vanity cabinets

Sink bases, drawer stacks, floating vanities with finished bottoms, linen towers. 21″ deep standard; heights to your drawing.

5 · HOODS & SHELVES

Hoods, shelves and open units

Wood hood fronts and chimneys built to the liner spec, floating shelves, open bookcase and display units with finished interiors.

6 · PANELS, FILLERS & TOE

Panels, fillers and toe kick

Finished end panels, appliance panels, back panels for islands, scribe fillers (1½″ fillers supplied at 3″ to scribe), toe kick and L-trim in the matching finish.

7 · MOLDINGS & ACCENTS

Moldings and accents

Crown, light rail, scribe, base and applied moldings to match your door, plus corbels, posts and decorative legs. Key West profiles available on request.

8 · ACCESSORIES

Interior accessories

Blum soft-close as standard. Roll-out trays, pantry pull-outs, cutlery and spice inserts, tray dividers, trash and recycling pull-outs, LeMans and lazy-susan corner units.

What ships

What's in the package

Each cabinet ships as finished, machined, pre-drilled components: sides, tops and bottoms, backs, fixed and adjustable shelves, stretchers where the design calls for them, doors and drawer fronts hung-ready with hinge cups bored, and ⅝″ maple dovetail drawer boxes assembled. Blum hinges, plates and drawer guides are included and counted on the approved package.

Finished end panels, fillers, toe kick, moldings and accessories ship with the kitchen on the same pallet whenever the load allows.
