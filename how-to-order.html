How to order

Send us your drawings. We return pricing, freight and numbered drawings for sign-off.

You don't have to configure every cabinet to get started. Most projects begin with a plan, a few elevations or even a measured sketch. We do the detailing.

Send your drawings

Plans, elevations, a designer's layout or a measured sketch, plus appliance specifications and your door, wood and finish direction. Use the request form, or email the files to the address below.

We price the package

Within a few business days you receive cabinet pricing for the package, destination freight, and the pallet count. Options you asked about are priced as separate lines so you can carve them in or out.

Numbered detail drawings

With pricing comes a set of numbered detail drawings: plan, elevations and a cabinet schedule showing every cabinet number, width, height, depth, door and drawer configuration and hardware. These are the specification of record.

Review and sign off

Mark up changes and we revise. When the drawings are right, you sign them and place the deposit. Nothing is cut until the drawings are signed.

Built and finished in Key West

Boxes are CNC-cut and machined, doors and fronts are built or sourced to your style, and everything is sprayed in our finish room. Lead time is stated on the quote.

Shipped ready to assemble

Packed on a consolidated pallet with like parts together and one bag each of dowels and screws, then shipped by freight to the address on the order. Balance is due before shipment.

Terms

Ordering terms

Deposit and balance
A deposit with signed drawings releases the order to production. The balance, including freight, is due before the shipment leaves Key West. Exact terms are stated on each quote.
Changes after sign-off
Changes after signed drawings are quoted as a revision and may move the ship date. Field dimensions and appliance specs are the buyer's responsibility.
Lead time
Lead time runs from signed drawings and deposit, not from the first inquiry, and is stated on the quote. Finish samples on new colors come first.
Who can order
Pricing and ordering run through trade accounts: cabinet shops, builders, remodelers, designers and installers. Homeowners are welcome to involve their professional, and we'll help you find one.
Warranty
Boxes, drawer boxes and finish are warranted against defects in material and workmanship; Blum hardware carries Blum's lifetime warranty. Warranty terms ship with the order.
