Construction

Quality inside. Beautifully finished outside.

These are the specifications every Key West Kitchens cabinet is built to. They carry straight over from Hansen & Bringle's full-custom work; nothing is lightened for the ready-to-assemble line.

Cabinet boxesFull ¾″ American-made maple plywood sides, tops, bottoms and fixed shelves. Pre-finished maple interior.
BacksFull plywood back, captured in a groove and glued. No hanging rails, no thin nailed-on back.
Base constructionFull tops on base cabinets. Sink and cooktop openings are cut to the approved drawings, not field-cut.
Cabinet systemFull-access Key West–style construction. No face frame; doors and drawer fronts overlay the box with consistent ⅛″ reveals.
Custom sizingWidth, height and depth specified in 1/16″ increments to your design, within each configuration's manufacturing limits.
Standard heightsBase: 30½″ box on a 4″ toe (34½″ to top of box). Wall: 30″, 36″, 42″ or to your drawing. Tall: 84″, 90″, 96″ or to your drawing.
Standard depthsBase 24″, wall 12″, tall 24″, vanity 21″. Any depth available to the drawing.
Adjustable shelves¾″ maple plywood, edgebanded, on 5mm metal shelf pins in 32mm line boring.
Drawer boxes⅝″ solid maple dovetail drawer boxes, clear-coated, ¼″ plywood bottoms.
Drawer guidesBlum full-extension undermount, soft close.
HingesBlum clip-top soft-close concealed hinges, 110° standard; wide-angle and bi-fold where the layout calls for it.
JoineryCNC-machined 8mm dowel construction with pre-drilled fastener locations. Finished ends attach with the same dowels plus concealed screws from inside the box.
Doors & drawer frontsYour door style in solid wood, MDF paint-grade or veneer; finished with the boxes so color matches across the whole kitchen.
FinishProfessionally sprayed in our Key West finish room before shipment: conversion-varnish paints, stains, clear coats and specialty finishes.
Toe kickSeparate 4″ toe kick in the matching finish, so bases set level on a ladder base or legs.

Why full-access

Why we build Key West style

Full-access cabinets give you the whole opening. Drawers are wider, interiors are easier to reach, and the finished look is a clean grid of doors and drawer fronts with even ⅛″ reveals.

It's the construction used in high-end European and contemporary American kitchens, and it's how Hansen & Bringle has built in the Keys for decades. In a salt-air climate a solid plywood box with a captured back and sprayed finish holds up better than a thin-backed stock cabinet.

Sizes, hardware and accessories for your project are set on the numbered detail drawings you approve before production. Those drawings are the specification of record.
