Assembly

Doweled, pre-drilled, and packed so the kitchen goes together in order.

Every part is CNC-machined with 8mm dowel holes and pre-drilled fastener locations, numbered to the drawing, and packed with like parts together. Assembly is glue, dowels, a mallet and a driver.

Stage by cabinet number

Pull the parts for one cabinet number from the pallet. Sides, top, bottom, back, shelves, doors and drawers all carry the same number from the approved drawings.

Dowel and glue the box

Glue the 8mm dowels into the pre-bored holes in the top and bottom, seat the sides, and square the box. One bag of dowels covers the whole kitchen.

Slide in the back, drive the screws

The full plywood back drops into its groove and squares the box. Confirmat screws go into the pre-drilled locations from inside the box. One bag of screws, no sorting.

Hang doors and set drawers

Blum hinge plates and drawer guides mount on pre-drilled 32mm positions. Clip on the doors, drop in the dovetail drawer boxes, snap on the drawer fronts.

Set, level and adjust

Set bases on the separate toe kick, level, fasten to the wall, then dial in the ⅛″ reveals with the Blum adjusters. Finished ends, fillers and moldings go on last.
