Species, paints and stains

20+ species. 1,600+ paint colors. Any stain you can match.

Wood species and grades. Maple, paint-grade maple, cherry, white and red oak, rift and quarter-sawn oak, walnut, hickory, alder, mahogany, cypress and more. Boxes are always maple plywood; the species choice is for doors, drawer fronts, finished ends and moldings.

Paint. 1,600+ colors matched to Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore decks, sprayed in catalyzed conversion varnish. Send a color name or chip and we match it.

Stains and clears. Standard and custom stains, glazes, wire-brushed and cerused finishes, and clear-coat natural finishes. Specialty finishes are quoted per project.

Physical finish samples are made before production on new colors and on every first project with a new trade partner. Screen colors are not a finish standard.
