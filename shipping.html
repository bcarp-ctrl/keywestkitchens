Shipping & receiving

Everything ships by freight, packed for the project.

Your cabinet components arrive organized on one large consolidated pallet with like parts grouped together and wrapped. Assembly fasteners come in one bag of dowels and one bag of screws. No carton-per-cabinet, no pile of hardware baggies.

One pallet, grouped by part

Sides with sides, tops and bottoms together, doors and drawer fronts face-protected, drawer boxes nested. Each part is numbered to the approved drawing.

Larger projects

Oversized kitchens and multi-room packages may take additional pallets. The pallet count, dimensions and weight are stated on your quote.

Freight quoted to your door

Freight is quoted for the actual shipment and destination. Routing, available capacity, order size and delivery access determine the final arrangement.

Key West made. A smarter trip out.

Why a Key West–made kitchen ships for a fair price.

Freight comes into Key West every day to supply the island. Those trucks need a return trip. By using return-trip freight capacity when it's available, we send a true Key West–made product beyond the island while keeping delivery costs practical for dealers and projects outside the Keys.

Every destination is quoted individually. Return-trip capacity is used when available; it is not a guarantee of a flat rate.

Receiving

Plan the delivery with your quote request.

Tell us on the pricing request where the shipment is going and how it will be received: a shop with a dock or forklift, a job site needing a liftgate, residential street access, gated communities, or an island or barge delivery. We quote the right service the first time.

Is unloading included?
Curbside freight delivery is standard. Liftgate service can be added on the quote. Unloading and inside placement are the receiver's responsibility unless arranged.
How do I check the shipment?
Count pallets and note any wrap damage on the driver's bill of lading before signing. Photograph anything questionable. Report concealed damage to us within 5 business days.
Where should the pallet be stored?
Indoors, dry and flat. Finished cabinetry should acclimate in conditioned space before assembly, the same as any fine millwork.
Do you ship outside Florida?
Yes. Freight is quoted anywhere in the continental United States; the Southeast and Gulf Coast see the best return-trip pricing.
