Trade program

Built for the professionals who build kitchens.

Key West Kitchens is sold trade-first. The website is public so your clients can see the kitchens, doors and finishes; pricing and ordering run through approved professional accounts, so you are never competing with us for your own customer.

Cabinet shops

Add a finished, custom-sized cabinet line without adding a CNC, a finish room or the crew to run them. Keep the design, the sale and the install.

Builders and remodelers

Offer a true custom package on every job while your team concentrates on the client, scheduling and installation.

Designers and installers

Specify to the 1/16″ and get numbered drawings back for sign-off. Your installer receives one organized pallet, not forty cartons.

New cabinet dealers

Enter the category with a real shop behind you. We start new partners with a physical finish sample and one manageable project.

How we work with partners

Clear responsibilities, from the first project on.

Every project has a signed set of numbered drawings. Field dimensions, appliance specifications and drawing approval sit with the trade partner; construction, finish, packing and freight to the agreed address sit with us. Assembly and installation are the partner's, with our assembly sheets, videos and phone support behind them.

Is there a minimum order?
No minimum. A single vanity ships on the same terms as a whole house, though freight is most efficient on a full pallet.
How is pricing shared?
Trade pricing is quoted per project to the account holder. We do not publish it or quote the end client directly.
Can my client visit?
Yes. Our Key West showroom at 3229 Flagler Ave, Unit 104 shows doors, finishes and completed work by appointment.
What about tops?
Hansen & Bringle fabricates natural and man-made stone and solid surface tops for Keys projects, and can template, fabricate and ship tops with the cabinet package where it makes sense.
